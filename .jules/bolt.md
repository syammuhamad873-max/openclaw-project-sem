## 2026-08-12 - Chunk Bundling Bloat & Route-Based Code Splitting
**Learning:** Large external charting packages like Recharts (which bundle robust components, rendering logic, and dependencies like d3, react-resize-detector, etc.) and comprehensive icon libraries like Lucide-React can inflate the main bundle enormously, slowing down initial TTI (Time to Interactive) and FCP (First Contentful Paint) for users. By employing dynamic imports with `React.lazy` and `React.Suspense`, we can defer loading of these dependencies until the specific routes they populate (such as Dashboard or Reports pages) are requested.
**Action:** Always identify routes or large sub-components containing heavy non-critical dependencies, and split them out using lazy loading to ensure the initial vendor/app JS chunk remains lightweight.
